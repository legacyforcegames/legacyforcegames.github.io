2. Weapon forging

The grade of a forged weapon depends on the difficulty you choose and your skill in the forging mini-game. Odds move evenly from the 0% skill row to the 100% skill row as your skill rises. The break chance falls the same way and is 0% when every step is perfect.

If fewer than 2 steps are perfect, the total chance of Legendary or higher is capped at 0.9%.

3. Promotion

Promoting a hero (from level 50) always raises the hero's grade by one. There is no chance of failure.
